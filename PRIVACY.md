# Privacy policy: Anamorphic De-Squeeze

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

Anamorphic De-Squeeze ("the app") is made by Jemish Mayani. It processes video entirely on your device.

**Data the app collects:** none. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

**Your files.** The app only reads the videos and LUT files you pick with Android's file picker. Videos are processed on your phone and saved to your Movies folder. Originals are never modified. Imported LUTs are stored privately inside the app and deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app.

**Crash and diagnostic reports** are stored only on your device. They are shared only if you copy one and send it yourself.

**Internet.** The Google Play version doesn't use the internet. The GitHub version connects only when you tap "Check for updates", to ask GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that request. Links such as the support page or source code open in your browser.

**Children.** The app isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes** to this policy will be published in this file.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/jemishmayani/Anamorphic-Desqueeze/issues
